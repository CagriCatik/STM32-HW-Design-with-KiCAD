# A


## ADC
Analog-to-Digital Converter. Converts an analog voltage into a digital value so the microcontroller can process sensor signals.

## Annotation

Assigning unique reference designators (R1, C2, U3 …) to every symbol in the schematic. The references link schematic, PCB, BOM and pick-and-place file.

## Annular Ring

The copper ring that remains around a drilled hole. Manufacturers specify a minimum (JLCPCB: about 0.1 mm for vias, 0.15 mm for plated through-hole pads); too small a ring can break out when the drill wanders.

## AP2112K

A 600 mA low-dropout 3.3 V regulator in SOT-23-5 with ~250 mV dropout and ~55 µA quiescent current. Used on the board instead of the AMS1117.
