# F


## Footprint

The physical layout of a component’s pads on the PCB.

## Ferrite Bead

An inductor with high losses at high frequency. Placed in series with the analog supply (VDDA) it blocks digital switching noise while passing DC.

## Freerouting

An open-source autorouter that reads Specctra DSN files exported from KiCad and writes the routed tracks back as a session (SES) file.

## Fiducial

A bare copper dot without solder mask that pick-and-place machines use as an optical reference to align the board.
