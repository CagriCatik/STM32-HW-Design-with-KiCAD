# M

## MCO (Microcontroller Clock Output)

A pin (PA8 on the STM32F103) that can output an internal clock, useful to verify the crystal with an oscilloscope.

## MPN (Manufacturer Part Number)

The manufacturer's own part number, for example AP2112K-3.3TRG1. Stored next to the LCSC number.

## MLCC

Multilayer ceramic capacitor, the standard SMD capacitor. X5R/X7R types lose a large part of their capacitance under DC bias; C0G types are stable and used for crystal load capacitors.
