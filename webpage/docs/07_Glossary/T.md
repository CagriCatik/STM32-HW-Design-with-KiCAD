# T

## Teardrop

A tapered copper fill where a track joins a pad or via. It reduces the risk of cracks and drill breakout.

## Thermal Relief

Spokes that connect a pad to a copper pour instead of a solid connection, so the pad can be soldered without the pour drawing away all the heat.

## TVS Diode

Transient Voltage Suppressor: a diode that clamps voltage spikes, for example on a battery input or USB lines.
